# leaf & lore — User Guide

A book store app for readers who love stories. Browse, wishlist, buy, and track your reading life.

---

## Getting started

### Create an account
1. Open the app
2. On the login screen, tap **Create an account**
3. Enter your name, email, and password (at least 6 characters)
4. Tap **Create account**

### Sign in
1. Enter your email and password
2. Tap **Sign in**

### Forgot your password?
1. On the login screen, tap **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email
3. Tap **Send reset link** — a confirmation appears

### Browse as a guest
You don't need an account to browse. Open the app and explore Home and Browse freely. You'll be asked to sign in when you try to add to cart, add to wishlist, or place an order.

---

## Browsing books

### Home
The Home screen shows:
- **Editor's picks** — curated selections
- **Browse by mood** — quick shortcuts by genre
- **Bestsellers** — top-rated books
- **New arrivals** — fresh releases
- **A little literary letter** — newsletter signup

### Browse
- **Genre chips** — tap All, Fiction, Mindfulness, Nature, Romance, Mystery, or Science
- **Search** — type in the field to search by title, author, or genre
- **Filters** — tap the Filters chip to pick a genre and set a price range
- **Sort** — tap Sort to reorder by popularity, newest, price, or rating

### Book details
Tap any book to see its cover, description, price, stock, and reader reviews.

---

## Saving books for later

### Wishlist
1. On any book card or details page, tap the **heart** icon
2. The heart fills red — the book is saved
3. Open the **Wishlist** tab to see all saved books
4. Tap **Add to cart** on any wishlist item to move it to your cart

---

## Buying books

### Add to cart
1. On Book Details, tap **Add to cart**
2. Adjust quantity with **−** and **+**
3. The cart badge in the top bar updates

### Cart
Open the **Cart** tab to see everything you've added. Adjust quantities, remove items, and view your order summary with subtotal, shipping, and total.

### Checkout (3 steps)
1. **Shipping** — pick a saved address or add a new one
2. **Payment** — pick a saved card or add a new one
3. **Review** — confirm items, address, payment, and total
4. Tap **Place order**

Shipping is free on orders over $50. Otherwise, it's $5.50.

### Order confirmation
After placing an order, you'll see the order number, total paid, and estimated delivery.

---

## Tracking your orders

### Order history
**Profile → My orders** shows every order you've placed with its status.

### Order details
Tap any order to see:
- Which books are in it
- Delivery address
- Payment method
- Order total

### Track order
From Order Details, tap **Track order** to see the 5-step timeline:
- Pending
- Processing
- Shipped
- Out for Delivery
- Delivered

## Managing your account

### Edit profile
**Profile → Edit profile** — update your name, email, or phone number.

### Addresses
**Profile → My addresses** — add, edit, or remove shipping addresses.

### Payment methods
**Profile → Payment methods** — add or remove saved cards. Only the last four digits are shown.

### Log out
**Profile → Log out** at the bottom.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need an account to browse?**  
No. You can explore Home and Browse freely as a guest. Sign in when you want to save books, add to cart, or place an order.

**How do I save a book?**  
Tap the heart icon on any book cover or on the Book Details page. Saved books appear in the Wishlist tab.

**Can I order without an account?**  
No. You need an account to place orders so we can save your order history.

**How do I track my order?**  
Profile → My orders → tap the order → Track order.

**Payment didn't go through. What now?**  
Check your saved card under Payment methods, or reach out to support.

**How do I get help?**  
Tap **Profile → Help & FAQ**, or email support@leafandlore.com.
